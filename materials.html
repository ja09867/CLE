How It Works

1. Laser Module

The laser module emits a focused light beam that engraves or cuts the surface of your chosen material.

2. Stepper Motors

Stepper motors move the laser along the X, Y, and Z axes with precision.

3. Control Board

The control board interprets commands from software and controls the laser and motors.

4. Software

LaserGRBL or LightBurn converts images into G-code for the machine.

5. Power Supply

Provides correct voltage and current to run everything smoothly.

6. Limit Switch

Defines boundaries, enables homing, and protects the machine.
