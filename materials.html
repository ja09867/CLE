⚠️ ARDUINO VOLTAGE WARNING — READ THIS: Arduino Uno accepts 7-12V on DC jack or 5V via USB.
DO NOT power Arduino through the CNC Shield's 12-36V input — it will bypass the regulator and DESTROY your board!
✅ SOLUTION: Use a dedicated 5V PSU OR add a buck converter to step down 12V/24V to 5V.

How It Works

1. Laser Module

The laser module emits a focused light beam that engraves or cuts the surface of your chosen material.

2. Stepper Motors

Stepper motors move the laser along the X, Y, and Z axes with precision.

3. Control Board

The control board interprets commands from software and controls the laser and motors.

4. Software

CLE Laser Control (or LaserGRBL / LightBurn) converts images into G-code for the machine.

5. Power Supply

Provides correct voltage and current to run everything smoothly.

6. Limit Switch

Defines boundaries, enables homing, and protects the machine.
